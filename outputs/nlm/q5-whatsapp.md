# Q5 — WhatsApp Commerce [SYNTHESIS — NLM timeout + Q1–Q3 2026-06-11]

Tag: SYNTHESIS

## List-dump ordering (Janta Stores model)
Housewives send a plain list of items in chat; staff responds with itemized bill + payment link. No website required — conversational commerce as primary channel.

## WhatsApp Business setup (Phase 1, ~50k budget)
- Dedicated WhatsApp number with manual operator for initial digital inquiries
- Full WhatsApp Business portfolio setup within engagement budget
- Foundation for CRM and order-taking in later phases

## WhatsApp Channels
For older generation / moms — new arrivals, seasonal products, updates. More trusted than website for target demographic.

## CRM segmentation
Agentic CRM tracks birthdays, anniversaries, preferences. Product-specific content (e.g. kaju benefits) pushed to relevant segments.

## Payment links
Post-order: performance bill + Razorpay/payment link in chat. Streamlined billing integrated with order management (Phase 3).

## Curated bundles
"Monthly Essentials", "Summer Fiesta", "Navratri Essentials" — increase AOV vs single-item orders.
