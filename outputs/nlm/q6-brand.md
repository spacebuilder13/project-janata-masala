# Q6 — Brand Positioning [SYNTHESIS — session 2026-06-11]

Tag: SYNTHESIS

## The "Janta" tension
"Janta" meaning masses is a constraint only if chasing generic premium. Stronger play: **heritage authority** — "this is where serious buyers come. Not because it's fancy. Because it's original."

## Framing
Think Sabyasachi, not Zara. Deeply rooted, elevated through craft and presentation. Gujarati-Kachchi community respects quality and demands it — different emotional register from aspirational premium.

## NLM-backed premium positioning
- Luxury-level art direction (Gucci-comparable references in sources)
- Sensory storytelling: spices crushed, vibrant haldi, product purity
- Unique USPs: 13mm kaju sizes not found elsewhere
- Respect existing heritage "aunty" logo while refining digital identity

## Mini brand revamp (Phase 1)
Branding book: refined logo, color palette, font palette, 10 mockups across touchpoints.
