The creative brainstorming session between the project team members (Sohum, 
Neyomi, and Utsav) led to several high-impact, low-budget ideas designed to 
transition Janata Masala into a modern, digitally-active brand.

### **Creative Content and Video Strategy**
To manage costs while maintaining a high-quality online presence, the team 
proposed moving away from generic agency-led posting in favor of a more 
authentic, process-driven approach:

*   **Workshop Approach for Phone Videos:** Instead of a full production crew 
for every post, the team suggested conducting a **workshop** with the client’s 
staff [1]. They would be taught how to shoot **"semi-aesthetic" videos** on 
their phones using specific templates provided by the team for **hooks, 
transitions, and cuts** [1].
*   **Sensory Content Focus:** The digital storytelling will focus on evocative,
high-production visuals that highlight product purity [2, 3]. Brainstorming 
ideas included showing **vibrant red chilies** or **turmeric (haldi) being 
crushed** into powder to create an emotional connection with the viewer [2, 4]. 
Content will also highlight unique USPs, such as specific **cashew (kaju) 
sizes** (e.g., 13mm) that are not commonly available elsewhere [1].

### **Branding and Packaging**
The team emphasized building a strong visual foundation to justify the brand's 
premium positioning:

*   **Mini Brand Revamp Elements:** For the initial phase, the team proposed a 
**cohesive branding book** [5, 6]. This includes a **refined logo** (while 
respecting the existing heritage logo), a specific **color palette**, a **font 
palette**, and **10 mockups** demonstrating the brand's use across different 
digital and physical touchpoints [5].
*   **Packaging Template:** To help with the client's private label expansion, 
the team discussed providing **one templatized packaging design** (for example, 
for dry fruits) [5, 7]. This template could then be easily duplicated by the 
client across various other product lines [5].

### **Marketing and Distribution Channels**
The strategy focuses on meeting the target audience where they are most 
comfortable:

*   **Performance Marketing for Ghatkopar Communities:** The team identified a 
"white space" in targeting the affluent, communal **Gujarati and Kachchi 
segments** in the **Ghatkopar belt** [8]. They proposed using **Meta 
(Instagram/Facebook) ads** to target housewives in these specific demographics 
repeatedly, driving them toward the store or the new digital ordering platforms 
[8].
*   **WhatsApp Channels for "Moms":** Recognizing that the older generation and 
housewives are highly active on WhatsApp, Neyomi suggested utilizing **WhatsApp 
channels** to share updates on new arrivals and seasonal products, as this 
demographic finds it more trustworthy and easier to navigate than a traditional 
website [9, 10]. 
*   **Curated "Essentials" Packages:** Another idea involved shifting away from 
single-item orders to **curated bundles** such as "Monthly Essentials," "Summer 
Fiesta," or "Navratri Essentials" to increase the average order value [3, 11].
