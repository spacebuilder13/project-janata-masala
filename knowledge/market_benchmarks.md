# Market Benchmarks — Janata Masala

Source: NLM Q3 (VERBATIM synthesis)

## Janta Stores (Borivali West)

- **Model:** Traditional kirana, back-end digitization not front-end
- **Channel:** Staff take orders via phone/WhatsApp, upsell, bill + payment link
- **Lesson for JM:** Conversational commerce; moms "dump" lists into chat

## CDS WPI (Charlie Daily Stores)

- **Model:** Day-to-day consumption, exclusive/imported goods
- **Positioning:** "Absolute customer delight," luxury feeling
- **Lesson for JM:** Premium USPs (13mm kaju), sensory storytelling, justify premium pricing

## Rupsub

- **Model:** Quick-commerce, geolocation-based
- **Tech:** Backend CRM + AI voice agents, 10–20 min delivery, consistent SOPs across 11 stores
- **Lesson for JM:** Tech backbone for scale; Phase 2/3 destination

## Stack model

CDS (how it looks) + Janta (how orders flow) + Rupsub (how it scales) = Janata Masala modernization path.
