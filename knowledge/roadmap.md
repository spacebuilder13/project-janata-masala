# Three-Phase Roadmap — Janata Masala

Source: NLM Q1 (VERBATIM synthesis)

## Phase 1: Branding & Instagram — ~4 weeks

- Digital identity: branding book, logo refinement, color/font palettes, 10 mockups
- Instagram: consistent high-quality content for discovery and awareness
- WhatsApp number with manual operator for initial digital inquiries
- Workshop approach for semi-aesthetic phone videos

## Phase 2: Foundational Systems — 4–6 weeks

- Inventory management, order intake, logistics modules
- Systems integration: financial, inventory, logistics "talk to each other"
- Replace pen-and-paper with documented workflows
- Goods lifecycle: storage → dispatch

## Phase 3: Digital Interface — 4–6 weeks

- Customer interface: WhatsApp-led conversational, website, or hybrid + AI voice
- Full Agentic CRM deployment
- Automated billing, payment links, real-time inventory for multiple locations
